## Intentional boundaries

The official form still has finite space. Exceptionally long particulars and scripts the bundled font/layout cannot safely render continue to receive actionable errors instead of silent truncation, missing glyphs or illegible text. The application prepares the form; the celebrant checks the evidence and completes the legal process. Mobile WebKit testing is emulation, not a physical iPhone test.

The address repair is server-side and accepts the existing form field names. A client whose form is still open can retry **Generate my NOIM PDF** without refreshing or re-entering their particulars.

For future releases, run `npm run check` and `npm run test:browser`, then verify the deployed journey and network traffic. Cloudflare documents the analytics-injection behaviour of `no-transform` in its [Web Analytics FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/faq/).
